# Contributing

Contributions to project-owned code and documentation are submitted under
the Mozilla Public License 2.0 in [LICENSE](LICENSE), unless explicitly
agreed otherwise before inclusion. You retain ownership of your contributions;
no copyright assignment is required.

Please submit only work you have the right to contribute under these terms.
Identify copied or adapted third-party material and preserve its notices.

Keep fixes focused, describe the behavior changed and how it was checked, and
avoid including personal captures, credentials or generated exports in patches.
Propose new dependencies before introducing them; bundled dependencies need a
review of their redistribution terms and an update to the release notices. As a
project principle though, they should be generally avoided.
